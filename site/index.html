      Methods

      How the numbers were made, and where they could mislead

      stats.nba.com via nba_api→raw CSV, cached→pandas features, tested for leakage→scikit-learn, numpy→pytest, 18 tests→static JSON→this page, Vercel

      Data

      Team game logs and season tables from stats.nba.com, 2000-01 to 2025-26 regular seasons. Shot zones and advanced stats for every season, player tracking from 2013-14, hustle from 2016-17. No player or play-by-play data, so nothing here measures individuals.

      Each season against itself

      Every season metric is z-scored within its own season before it is compared with winning, so the three-point boom cannot pose as an edge. The six-factor table centers each rate on that season's league average for the same reason.

      Scored on unseen seasons

      Pre-game models train on 2000-01 to 2022-23 and are scored on the three seasons after, then walked forward a season at a time. Season-level models leave one whole season out at a time.

      Intervals that respect seasons

      Confidence intervals resample whole seasons, not games, because games in one season share rules, schedules and rosters. Correlations are compared with a chance ceiling from 1,000 shuffles of win % within each season.

      Chosen after looking

      Some comparisons were picked after the results were in: the four-factor model in the accuracy ladder and the walk-forward gap. Those margins are optimistic and are labeled where they appear. Betting lines are not included and would likely beat every model.

      Reproduce it

      One command rebuilds every number on this page from the raw tables: uv run python -m nbawin.run, then nbawin.dashboard. Eighteen tests, including one that plants a signal and checks the atlas finds it and ignores shuffled labels.
