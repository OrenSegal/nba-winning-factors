9Does it hold up, season after season?

Walk-forward accuracy: each season scored by models trained only on earlier seasons

How this was made. Team game logs from stats.nba.com, 2000-01 to 2025-26 regular seasons, validated on load. Every factor is computed only from games played before tip-off, and a test perturbs a game and every later game to prove nothing leaks. Training seasons are 2000-01 to 2022-23; 2023-24 to 2025-26 are held out. Bootstrap intervals treat games as independent, which understates uncertainty a little.

What it cannot see. Injuries, lineups, minutes, referees, betting lines. Those would move forecasts more than anything here. Full technical report.
